# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People in India exploring government financial schemes, tax choices, property costs, and household financial decisions.

## Product Purpose

ADITI helps visitors identify relevant public schemes and understand everyday financial choices through a profile matcher, calculators, and short learning exercises.

## Positioning

An approachable single workspace that connects eligibility discovery with plain-language financial education.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The frontend relies on the existing matching, explanation, and subscription API endpoints. Eligibility results and calculations are informational aids, not financial or legal advice.

## Brand Commitments

Inferred from the redesign brief: the product should feel aesthetic, future-facing, calm, and trustworthy without obscuring familiar financial tasks.

## Evidence on Hand

Scheme data, matching logic, calculators, quiz content, and API integration are present in the repository. No verified third-party proof or customer claims are available.

## Product Principles

- Make the next action unmistakable.
- Use plain, humane language for consequential choices.
- Preserve clarity and access over spectacle.
- Keep educational and eligibility tools in one focused place.
