# ADITI visual system

## Direction

Warm financial companion: a calm, gently rounded workspace inspired by Claude's editorial typography and conversational restraint, without copying its identity.

## Color and material

Warm paper surfaces dominate. Plum creates trust and depth; bright coral carries primary action, marigold signals value, and petal pink/mint create small, optimistic moments.

## Type and layout

DM Sans carries UI text while Newsreader supplies gentle editorial display type. A compact rail becomes scrolling navigation on mobile; task panels stay full-width and legible.

## Components and motion

Panels use 18–26px radii, fine warm borders, and soft shadows. Controls have visible focus, hover, and disabled states; decoration is kept intentionally quiet.
